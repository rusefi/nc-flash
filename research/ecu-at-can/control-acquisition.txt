Bounded acquisition slice
------------------------
4B80..4C94 executes32 original word reads, in channel order, and copies to
RAM4008+2*channel. Result registers are F800..F816, F820..F836 and F840..F84E,
all withFFFF prefix. The slice ends before the enclosing epilogue. It does
not execute conversion start/status waits, initialization or an interrupt.
Each register read must have an explicit sample and correct16-bit width;
missing samples, byte reads, status-register access and result writes fail.
Existing SCI/application peripheral support remains inherited and unchanged.

Harness attachment retains the existing CPU/RAM/serial state and specializes
only the result-register interface. There is no instruction replacement,
conversion clock, status generation, voltage model or external controller.
100 arbitrary-word vectors test copying/decoding;1,024 valid10-bit vectors
cover all counts, simultaneously on channels1 and8. Arbitrary low-six-bit
vectors are bit-handling tests, not claimed physical conversion results.
