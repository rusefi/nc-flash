Feedback-driven override: full 21D18
-----------------------------------
FFFF prefixes below identify ECU RAM. R(x) is round-toward-zero binary32.
Input C is protected closed value 2114 read with 15246, falling back to stock
BADD8=10.5 on invalid checksums. F is feedback 5534, A is candidate angle569C,
M is byte566E, P is previous mode552C, O is protected output5520.
The 154BC reads of F and O do not validate their protected checksums here.

High threshold H=R(C+BB188); low threshold L=R(H-BB18C).
552D becomes1 when F>=H, becomes0 when F<L, otherwise retains its value.
Stock BB188=.5 and BB18C=0 make both thresholds equal: this stock image has
no nonzero hysteresis band. Equality takes the high branch.

If M!=1: O=0 and both counters5528/552A clear.
If M==1 and P==0 (entry):
  I=R(C+BB17C), stock BB17C=.5.
  O=I when F>=I; otherwise O=min(L,R(A+C)).
  Both counters retain their previous values on this entry path.
If M==1 and P!=0 (retained mode):
  Select counter5528 when 552D==1, otherwise552A.
  If its OLD value>=its stock threshold2, subtract a rounded step from O
  and clear that counter, then increment it. Clear the opposite counter.
  Both step constants BB184/BB190 are binary32 approximately .05.
  O=max(stock BB180=5, resulting O).
Finally 15522 writes O with both checksum words, and 552C=M.

With zero entry counters, the third retained-mode call makes the first
reduction; reductions then occur every two calls while that side persists.
Switching threshold sides resets the opposite counter. These are calls, not
milliseconds. Entry has no floor5; that floor applies to the retained branch.
A non-Boolean previous mode also counts as retained, but only current M==1
admits this override. Tests cover mode/history0/1/2, equality, floor, timing
edges and high-bit counter values, plus protected closed-value fallback.

Previously verified25682 selects5520 only when566C!=1 and566E==1, then clamps
