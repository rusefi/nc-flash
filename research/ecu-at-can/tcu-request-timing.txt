RESULT AND LIMIT

Original timer-wheel, mode-gate, event-dispatch, queue and request-state code
now execute together. Ramp timers8115..8120 increment on eight of sixteen
primary-wheel slots; hold timers81F2..81FD increment on two. Manager group5
receives service on four of eight admitted periodic calls. These are distinct
clocks. Timer expiry alone does not release a request: the next manager
service evaluates expiry, releases it and drains queued cleanup.

This establishes instruction-level call ratios, not milliseconds. The full
hardware ISR and application task have NOT executed in these fixtures. The
coupled tests deliberately vary their call ratio and initial timer phase.
Do not mistake their tick column for measured time or firmware scheduling.
No ROM, application code or shared interpreter was changed.
