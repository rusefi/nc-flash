VERIFIED DOCUMENTATION (not firmware execution or vehicle results)
Printed09-16-18/19, PDFpages63/64(one-based): simultaneous switches while
stopped inhibit motion. At either endpoint, holding its ineffective direction
and then pressing the other also inhibits motion. During opening or closing,
pressing the opposite switch while retaining the first stops motion and
inhibits both inputs. A subsequent operation requires release detection.
This settles the previous flattened-table ambiguity; it does not establish
arbitrary intermediate-position reversal after release.
Printed09-16-8, PDFpage53: closing chart depicts switch-release pause during
deck opening and resumption after another close press. Later ignition-off
pauses roof closing; ignition-on followed by a new close press resumes.
Printed09-16-4, PDFpage49: opening explicitly shares the pause behavior.
Both charts sequence deck opening, roof movement, then deck closing, and show
the deck-close switch OFF at the closed endpoint. Neither chart supplies a
numerical CAN timeout, pulse mismatch limit or complete reversal matrix.
