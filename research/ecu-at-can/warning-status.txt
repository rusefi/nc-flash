Warning-status endpoints: original-code evidence, 2026-10-05

Scope and inputs

This extends fault-reporting.txt using the same unchanged, SHA256-checked
LFFEEE-stock.bin ECU and LFG1TF000.bin TCU. Hexadecimal addresses, zero-based
payload bytes; RAM addresses have FFFF prefix. It distinguishes stored MIL
request from the active diagnostic indication. Neither is proof of mechanical
fallback or a physical cluster lamp. Traction/roof goals remain open.

TCU stored flag -> diagnostic MIL status and CAN216

5434C builds PID01 body: 01, count+(A99B!=0 ?80:00),04,00,00.
54A36 computes the stored-DTC count from6188 and group codes/filter582F4.
The thirteen eight-byte entries at5E778 include PID01 at5E780 pointing to5434C.
54284 dispatches requested PIDs; outer54234 checks mode01 permission through
5604C. Synthetic90B6=1 admits the request; default0 did not. The physical
protocol state and transport framing have not been executed.

Primary semantic reference: Elm Electronics ELM327 datasheet, printed p36,
https://elmelectronics.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/05/ELM327DSL.pdf
Accessed2026-10-05. Its mode01/PID01 example identifies first data-byte bit7
as MIL status and the lower seven bits as stored-DTC count. That definition,
together with the actual dispatcher/handler, supports naming A99B as the
TCU's stored MIL request. It is also CAN216 byte7 bit1 (18F10/18F8C).

verify_fault_reporting.py executes54234 with a one-PID request context.
Before qualification the body is01 00 04 00 00; with storedC100/U0100 it is
01 81 04 00 00. It stays81 after active recovery in all fourteen tested
lifecycles. These are PID bodies, without positive service41 or ISO-TP header.
Stored-flag aging/clear remains untraced; persistence across power loss is
not established by synthetic RAM execution.

ECU request -> CAN420

The previously verified chain is CAN216 byte7 bit1 -> protected2184 ->6E53
via3B282 -> protected24EE via775CA. 7774A then selects input1 only if91BD==0
and (protected24EE==1 OR6536==0). It calls8B8A0 and stores its result in91BE.
The physical meanings of91BD and6536 remain unresolved.

8B8A0 uses the16-byte descriptorAD46C: maskE0 at+8 and pointerFFFF960C
at+12. If maskE0 AND966C is zero, mode960C is cleared. Mode0 passes input
and updates9638=(input==1); mode7 returns(9638==1); other modes pass input.
This diagnostic override is separate from the upstream9462 inhibit.

371F0 builds6B8C from91BD==1 atbit15,91BE==1 atbit14,693A==1 atbit6.
CAN420 descriptor379DC has DLC8 and buffer6B80. Transmitter3707A packs:
  byte0=6B88, byte1=6B89, bytes2..3=BE16[6B8A], byte4=6B8F,
  bytes5..6=BE16[6B8C], byte7=6B8E.
Thus the gated MIL request reaches CAN420 byte5 bit6. The TCU's420 receiver
copies only byte0; it does not consume this warning field through that copy.

Actual7774A/8B8A0/371F0 plus packing instructions370A6..370DC are executed.
The complete3707A attempt stops at an inexact float multiply at2F326 in the
preceding counter conversion. The exact-only FPU harness correctly rejects
it, even with zero counter RAM. No ROM or helper was mocked. The saved test
therefore explicitly executes the straight packing slice, not full transmit
scheduling or the6A48 peripheral driver. 7778E/777A0 are clear/reset paths
for protected24EE, not ordinary readers as a literal-reference list alone
could suggest. Cluster reception and lamp hardware remain outside the test.

TCU active aggregate -> CAN231

A664=A665=1,8464=0,A667=A668=0. Selection5329A compares unsigned16[8464]
