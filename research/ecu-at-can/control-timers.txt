Protected RAM side condition discovered during the long test
------------------------------------------------------------
The first mode-transition expectation was too broad: mode1 remained rejected
because5354 was1 and protected2110 was0. Tracing original15146 ->157F0 showed
5354 is set by invalid protected-byte reads. In the sparse fixture, uninitialized
210C,2076,2088,208A are00/00 instead of valid00/FF. The read uses its supplied
default, records the offending address at534C and sets5354. That side effect
is separate from SCI1 missing-feedback fault20A8 and must not be attributed
to real serial loss. The full RAM-initialization/diagnostic task is unmodeled.

The retained mode1 test now CHECKS three rejected calls with5354=1/2110=0,
then explicitly supplies valid protected2110=1 at step4 to exercise the known
conditional-admission path through counter624. A separate comparison supplies
valid zero records at the four addresses before mode1:5354 stays0,2110 stays0,
and selection is normal,feedback,feedback across three actual paired cycles.
No diagnostic flag is patched or cleared in either comparison. This correction
preserves the observed failure and identifies its fixture cause instead of
presenting it as a vehicle mode-transition requirement.
