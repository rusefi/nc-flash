Primary-source interpretation and scope correction
SAE International's public J1979_199709 summary identifies Mode04 as clearing
emission-related diagnostic information:
https://saemobilus.sae.org/standards/j1979_199709-e-e-diagnostic-test-modes
Exact short excerpt, provenance and limitations are saved in
sources/sae-j1979-mode04-source.txt. The full standard was not retrieved.
Together with firmware service4 permission, row3 header44 and wrapper53B5C,
this supports interpreting this caller as a diagnostic-clear request. It does
not establish complete diagnostic reset coverage or EEPROM persistence.
The9415 pulse also has the separate startup-edge trigger already executed in
tcu-inhibit-writers.txt and tcu-pulse-input.txt; do not name all its uses solely
from the diagnostic caller. Do not present this one-bit diagnostic request as
a normal operational shift-command queue or claim it closes those queue gaps.
