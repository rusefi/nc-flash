3F97C reads factor7154 and helper2118 compares it against +/-epsilon,
where epsilon ROM3FBC8 bits3727C5AC = approximately0.00001.
  abs(factor)<=epsilon:715C=protected711C.

helper converts its argument to unsigned8, so do not extend the formula to
arbitrary counts. The count's producer and physical meaning remain open.

3FA3E holds7174 unless71A8 is positive and outside a deadband of approximately
0.00078125 (ROM3FC00 bits3A4CCCCC). Otherwise it computes:
  q=max(71B0-7170+protected71D0+protected71D8,0)/71A8
  7174=71AC - approximate_sqrt(q).
Its own loop grows a power-of-two trial bound, then accumulates decreasing
steps whose squares do not exceedq, halving until step<=ROM3FC10=0.17578125.
This is firmware's iterative quadratic-model inversion, not an FPU sqrt.
For the tested q range0..64, it equals floor(4*sqrt(q))/4; negative numerator
clamps to0.69 cases cover these values and deadband/nonpositive holds.
Upstream coefficient producers remain outside these synthetic-input tests.
