Hall supply <=5V or motor supply reduction stops motors; recovery requires
ignition OFF/ON. Missing Hall pulses temporarily stop motion; another switch
operation permits retry, with pulse detection listed as recovery. Excessive
left/right pulse-count difference stops either roof or deck motors. Retry uses
the switch; after at least three detections, recovery requires ignition OFF/ON.
The numerical mismatch threshold is unspecified. Invalid limit-switch logic
inhibits operation until normal logic returns. Window-notification faults
have asymmetric closing/folding restrictions, including a final permitted
close; refer to the table and existing U0030/U0031 evidence before modeling.

ignition LOCK and battery disconnection. Therefore a recovery action and
clearing diagnostic memory are distinct concepts.
