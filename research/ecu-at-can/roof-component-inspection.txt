Both describe pulse range0..13V and an internal PTC thermal condition that can
prevent rotation, followed by approximately3 minutes cooling before reinspection.
This is a component inspection/retest interval, NOT a CAN timeout, firmware
restart delay, auto-resume guarantee or measured thermal threshold. Actual
recovery in both opening and closing remains unverified.

between roof and deck motors, or equate componentA with module3A.

Corroborated references / limitations
------------------------------------
The retained2008 module table provides opposing open/close drive references
for module motor pairs: roofRH3A/3I,LH3L/3D; deckRH3M/3E,LH3H/3P.
First in each pair is high for opening, second for closing; opposite leg
normally <=1V; other-condition reference about2V. Its footnote allows the low
leg voltage to rise during speed control. This differs from duplicate-polarity
entries in the2006 training table. It is a factory diagnostic reference, not
waveform, firmware, duty-cycle or physical-actuation proof.
The same table's deck-close3O is B+ at closed, <=1V elsewhere, unlike3F/1R/1T.
