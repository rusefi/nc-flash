-------------------

18A44 independent model (all arithmetic widths follow original opcodes):

 StorewordM->8A3C+j. E=s16(s16(A5E4+j)-s16(storedM))->8A34+j.

 -s16(D)*1000 then+s16(D)*1000. Negative bounds are not reordered here.
 StoreI, correction=trunc(I/1000).32-bit addition wraps beforecomparisons.

register for the chosen argument; reference preserves that distinction.

(printed206/PDFzero245) identifies F510/F512/F514/F516 as BFR6A/B/C/D.

configuration still determine actual behavior. SH7055S-compatible register
