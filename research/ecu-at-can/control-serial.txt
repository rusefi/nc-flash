F008..F00E name SMR1,BRR1,SCR1,TDR1,SSR1,RDR1,SDCR1; F746 isPDDR.
SMR=80 selects synchronous operation. ED1A is interrupt status. This names

Bounded ControlSerial harness
-----------------------------
ExtendsSHControlFloat locally in the verifier. Allows only byteF008..F00E,
wordF746/ED1A, allwithFFFF prefix. Config/port accesses are fixtures plus
logged writes; SSR reads return an explicit sample, status writes are logged
rather than treated as RAM. Each RDR read consumes one supplied byte and
fails if absent. Wrongwidth/unknownaccess/RDRwrite fail. TDRwrites are captured.
No clock, electrical transfer, peripheral interrupt generation or external
controller is simulated. Calls to E86E are explicit, not a complete ISR run.
SRmaskF0 avoids unrelated scheduler tail. No unbounded or MMIO-wide allowance.
