FACTORY CORRELATION AND LIMITS

https://www.ncmiata-servicemanual.com/src/g5w05/html/id050208801600.html
The factory procedure calls P0722 a vehicle-speed-sensor circuit malfunction
and describes D-range, turbine pulses and absent VSS signal as detection
conditions. It identifies VSS B/A connections to TCM2D/2C and says the code
can be stored with first-drive-cycle warnings; it lists no pending code.

but does not identify the MCU pins or prove that this firmware matches the
manual's calibration. The manual's12-pulse description and this image's
separate >12 / >6000 states must not be flattened into one threshold.
